MariaDB tips
Use bind-address = 0.0.0.0 to allow remote connections.
Tune innodb_buffer_pool_size (roughly 70% of RAM) for performance.
Check the error logs if the service fails to start.

MariaDB cannot run two major versions side by side from the operating system’s packages, so CelikPanel offers no second MariaDB and no move between versions; a MariaDB major change converts data in place and is not offered.
