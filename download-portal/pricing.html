3

When a licence ends

A licence ends on its end date. From version 1.0, reminders start one month before: 30, 7 and 1 day before the end date, in the panel and by e-mail.

Without a licence, the panel’s screens are closed until the licence is renewed or a new one is entered; entering a licence and installing the panel’s own updates stay possible. Your sites, mail, DNS and databases keep running.

4

What never depends on the licence

No licence state, lapse or unreachable licence service stops, removes or changes a hosted site, DNS, mail, database, scheduled job or certificate renewal: they are the server’s own services. Panel updates stay available in every licence state.
