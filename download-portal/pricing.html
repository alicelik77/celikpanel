Licence: per server, per year

One licence per server; accounts, domains and sites are not counted. Free during the alpha. The terms from version 1.0 are decided; the payment provider and tax details are not.

1

Today, during the alpha

What the licence does now.

Table 1 · The licence today
PriceFree
TermOne year from when you create it; renewing is free, from your account
ChecksWhile in use, the panel checks its licence about once an hour
If celikpanel.net cannot be reachedManagement stays open for up to 7 days
When a licence endsAlmost every panel screen locks until it is renewed. Sites, mail, DNS, databases and scheduled jobs keep running; they never consult the licence.
2

Terms from version 1.0

Decided by the owner on 14 November 2026. Payment starts with version 1.0.

Table 2 · The licence from version 1.0
Price99 USD per server per year (list price)
What is countedNothing: accounts, domains and sites are unlimited. One edition with every feature.
Price lockThe price you pay does not rise while you renew without a break.
Reminders30, 7 and 1 day before the end date, in the panel and by e-mail.
After the end date30 days of grace with everything working and a notice on top.
ThenOnly growth closes: creating a domain, an account, a mailbox and the like. Sites, mail, DNS, databases, scheduled backups, certificate renewal, panel and system security updates, the firewall, the security emergency and downloading backups stay available.
If celikpanel.net cannot be reachedThe panel keeps working fully for 30 days on its last successful check. One server’s licence never blocks another server.
Moving to another serverFrom your account, free, as often as needed; one active server per licence.
Trial30 days, without a card.
RefundWithin 30 days of buying, without conditions.
Automatic renewalOnly if you choose it, with a reminder 7 days before.
Lifetime licencesNone.
Beta participantsTheir first paid year is free, and they keep a founder price; its amount is not decided.

What never depends on the licence

No licence state, lapse, refund or unreachable licence service stops, removes or changes a hosted site, DNS, mail, database, scheduled job or certificate renewal. Panel updates stay available in every licence state.
