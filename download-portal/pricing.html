Questions before you buy.

Short answers. The full terms are written into the product’s decision record.

Does anything on my server stop when a licence ends?

No. Sites, mail, DNS, databases, backups, certificate renewal, updates and security keep working at every step. From version 1.0, after 30 days of grace only creating new domains, accounts and mailboxes waits for a renewal.

What counts as a server?

One machine running the panel. A licence belongs to one active server at a time; a pair of two servers takes two licences.

Do I pay per account, domain or site?

No. Nothing is counted. One licence per server covers everything on it.

Will my price go up?

Not while you renew without a break. The price you pay stays the price you pay.

I am moving to a new server. What happens?

From version 1.0 you move the licence to the new server yourself, from your account, for free, as often as you need.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The 30-day trial needs no card. Today, during the alpha, there is nothing to pay at all.

What if your licence service cannot be reached?

From version 1.0 the panel keeps working fully for 30 days on its last successful check; today that window is seven days. Your hosted services never depend on it.

How will I pay?

Payment opens with version 1.0, in US dollars. The payment provider is not chosen yet.
