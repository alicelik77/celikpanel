CelikPanel runs your websites, mail, domains and databases on a server. This chapter shows where things are on the screen, what your role lets you do, and where to start.

## Signing in {#signing-in}

Open the panel's address in your browser. Whoever gave you your account sends you this address, your **Username** and either a password or a link to set one.

- If you turned on two-factor authentication, the panel asks for the six-digit code from your authenticator app after your password.
- If you forgot your password, choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. A reset link is sent to your account's email address.

:::note
CelikPanel never asks for your password by email or by phone. Type it only on your panel's own sign-in page.
:::

## The screen {#the-screen}

- **The menu** on the left holds everything you manage, grouped under **Hosting** and, for the administrator, **Server**. It can be folded down to icons with **Collapse menu**. On a phone, open it with the **Menu** button at the top left.
- **The page** in the middle shows one thing at a time: a list, a domain, a component.
- **The top right** has the help button (**?**), the language, the look of the panel, and your own menu with your name on it.

## Who can do what {#roles}

Every account has one role. The menu shows only what your role can use, and the server checks every change again, whatever the screen shows.

| Role | Manages |
|---|---|
| Administrator | The whole server: components, security, updates, every account. |
| Reseller | Their own customers, the plans they sell, and their own sites. |
| Customer | Their own domains, sites, mail, databases and backups, within their plan. |
| User | A team member added by a customer. Sees only the domains and the parts the customer opened for them. |

What your plan includes, for example whether you may use SSH or how many mailboxes you may create, is set by whoever provides your account.

## Your first steps {#first-steps}

- **Administrator:** finish **Server setup**, check **Components**, then add the first domain. The server setup screen says what is still missing and why.
- **Reseller:** open **Users** to create a plan and your first customer.
- **Customer:** open **Domains**, choose your domain, and look at its sections: **Site** for the website, **Mail** for mailboxes, **Files** for the site's files.
- **User:** open **Domains**. You see the domains your team opened for you.

## Your account {#your-account}

Your own menu at the top right opens **My account**. There you change your password and your email address, and turn on two-factor authentication. A team member changes the password from the same menu, with **Change password**. **Sign out** is at the bottom of the menu.

## Getting help {#help}

- The **?** at the top right opens the guide at the part that explains the page you are on.
- **User guide** in the same menu shows every chapter for your role, with search.
- **Take the tour** shows the main parts of the screen again, in a few steps.

When a screen stops you, it says why, who has to act, and what to do next. [Reading a state and getting help](guide:reading-states) explains how to read those messages.
